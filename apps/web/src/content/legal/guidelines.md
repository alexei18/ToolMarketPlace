Tryworthy works when listings are real and feedback is honest. These rules apply to product listings, perks, feedback, roadmap ideas, profiles and reports.

## Not allowed in listings

- **Spam:** the same product listed twice, listings that only exist to send traffic elsewhere, or keyword stuffing.
- **Scams and misleading listings:** fake products, false claims about features or pricing, and products that take payment without delivering.
- **Malware:** software or websites that harm devices, steal data or install anything without clear consent.
- **Illegal content:** anything illegal where it is offered, including counterfeit goods and services that break consumer, privacy or intellectual property law.
- **Copied products:** listing someone else's product, or a copy that uses their name, design or content without permission.
- **Harmful content:** content that promotes violence, hatred, harassment or the sexual exploitation of anyone.

## Not allowed in feedback and roadmaps

- Fake feedback, including feedback written by makers about their own products or paid for by anyone.
- Abuse, harassment, threats or personal data about other people.
- Advertising for other products and repeated off-topic posts.

## Fair ranking

- Don't create or use extra accounts to save, claim, click, vote or give feedback.
- Don't buy, sell or trade saves, votes, feedback or claims.
- Don't use bots or scripts on the site.

## Reports

Reports help us find problems. Use them honestly:

- Report a product only when you believe it breaks these rules or the law.
- Don't report a product to hurt a competitor or to get back at a maker.
- False or abusive reports can lead to limits on your account.

To report something, use "Report this product" on the product page, or see [Report content](/report).

## What happens when rules are broken

Depending on how serious it is, we can hide or remove the content, lower a product's position, remove a perk, limit features or suspend the account. Serious or repeated problems lead to a permanent ban. You can contest our decisions as described in the [Terms of Service](/terms#contesting-a-decision).
