Anyone can report content on Tryworthy that they believe is illegal or breaks our [Community Guidelines](/guidelines), with or without an account.

## What you can report

- Illegal content, including counterfeit goods or services
- Intellectual property infringement, such as a copied product or a stolen logo
- Scams and fraud
- Malware or harmful websites
- Spam, misleading listings, fake feedback or perks that aren't honoured

## How to report

**With an account:** open the product page, choose "Report this product", pick a reason and add a note.

**Without an account:** use the form at the end of this page. Your email is optional; without it we can't tell you what we decided.

**For anything that isn't a product (feedback or a profile):** email [DSA_CONTACT_EMAIL] with:

1. the link to the content (product page, feedback or profile);
2. the reason (illegal content, intellectual property, scam or fraud, malware, or other);
3. an explanation of why you believe the content is illegal or breaks our rules, with any evidence;
4. your name and email address, unless you are reporting certain serious crimes such as child sexual abuse material, where you can stay anonymous;
5. a statement that you believe in good faith that the information in your report is accurate and complete.

## What happens next

- We confirm that we received your report, on screen and by email if you gave one.
- A person reviews it. We may hide the content while we check it.
- We tell you what we decided and why, if you gave us your contact details.
- The person who posted the content is told what we did and why, and can contest it, as described in the [Terms of Service](/terms#contesting-a-decision).

## Please report honestly

Reports that are knowingly false or made to hurt a competitor break our [Community Guidelines](/guidelines) and can lead to limits on your account.

## Authorities

Authorities and trusted flaggers can contact us at [DSA_CONTACT_EMAIL], in English or Romanian.
