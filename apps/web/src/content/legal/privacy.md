## What we collect and why

For each activity below you'll find the data, why we need it, the legal basis and how long we keep it. "Contract" means the processing is needed to give you the service you signed up for (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR and the matching basis in Law no. 195/2024). "Legitimate interest" means we need it to run a fair and safe service, and we have checked that it doesn't override your rights (Article 6(1)(f)).

### The waitlist

- **Data:** your email address; whether you want to discover apps or build them; the categories you pick (up to 5), or your product's link and category; your answer to "Where did you find us?" if you give one; the campaign tags of the link that brought you (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`); your personal share code and, if you came through someone's share link, whose link it was; and when you joined, confirmed, got an invite or unsubscribed. We don't store your IP address. Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you are not a bot and sign-ups are rate-limited by IP address, as described under [Signing in and security](#signing-in-and-security).
- **Why:** to confirm your email address, send makers their early-access invite and everyone else one invite on launch day with the top picks in their categories, show you how many people joined through your share link, and know which videos and campaigns bring people. The person whose link you used sees only a number, never who joined.
- **Legal basis:** consent for the emails, which you give by confirming your email address and can withdraw at any time with the unsubscribe link in every email; legitimate interest for the campaign tags, the "Where did you find us?" answer and the share-link counts.
- **Kept:** if you don't confirm, your sign-up is deleted 7 days after we send the confirmation email. If you unsubscribe, we keep the record that you did, so we don't email you again. All waitlist data is deleted 6 months after launch. If you create an account, your campaign tags and categories are copied to it when you sign up and kept as described below.

### Your account

- **Data:** your name, email address, password (stored only as a one-way hash), whether you confirmed your email, username, bio, profile picture and the date you joined. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile picture and Google account ID, and we store the sign-in tokens Google returns.
- **Why:** to create your account, let you sign in and show your public profile. Your name, username, picture and bio appear on your profile and next to public feedback you write.
- **Legal basis:** contract.
- **Kept:** until you delete your account. When you delete it, we delete everything tied to it except public feedback, which stays with the author shown as "Deleted user". We keep only the date and time of the deletion.

### Where you came from

- **Data:** if the link that brought you to Tryworthy had campaign tags (`utm_source`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`, for example from one of our videos), we save them on your account when you sign up. Only the first ones count. Until then they travel in the page address only; we don't store them in your browser. If you joined the waitlist first, the tags saved with your sign-up are copied to your account.
- **Why:** to know which videos and campaigns bring people who sign up.
- **Legal basis:** legitimate interest.
- **Kept:** until you delete your account. They are included in your data export.

### Signing in and security

- **Data:** for each signed-in browser, your IP address, browser type (user agent) and when the session expires. When you join the waitlist, create an account, claim a perk, report a product or start publishing one, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you are not a bot; it receives your IP address and technical details about your browser. Requests are rate-limited by IP address.
- **Why:** to keep you signed in, stop automated sign-ups and spot misuse.
- **Legal basis:** contract for sessions; legitimate interest for bot checks and rate limits.
- **Kept:** a session ends 30 days after you last used it and its record is deleted the following day. Email confirmation links expire after 24 hours and password reset links after one hour. Expired links, and any link older than 24 hours, are deleted in a daily cleanup. Rate-limit counters are not stored by us.

### Your feed and notifications

- **Data:** the categories you follow, how often you want the digest, the notifications we create for you (the digest, replies to your feedback, roadmap updates and service messages) and, if you turn on push notifications, the push subscription your browser creates (an address at your browser's push service and two encryption keys).
- **Why:** to build your feed and tell you about new products and replies.
- **Legal basis:** contract; consent for push notifications, which you give in your browser and can withdraw at any time in [Settings](/settings) or in your browser.
- **Kept:** your categories until you change them or delete your account. Read notifications are deleted after 90 days; unread ones stay until you read them or delete your account. A push subscription is deleted when you turn push off or when your browser's push service tells us it has expired.

### What you do on product pages

- **Data:** the products you save, the perks you claim (which perk, the code you received and when), the feedback you send (its type, text and whether it is public), your roadmap votes and any reports you file (reason and note).
- **Why:** to give you these features. Makers see the feedback sent to their products, with your name. Public feedback is visible to everyone. Makers see how many people claimed a perk, not who.
- **Legal basis:** contract; legitimate interest for reports, which we use to keep the site safe.
- **Kept:** until you remove them (saves and votes) or delete your account.

### Reports without an account

- **Data:** the product link, the reason and explanation you give, your email address if you add one, and that you confirmed the good-faith statement. Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you are not a bot.
- **Why:** to review reports of illegal content and rule-breaking listings, confirm that we received yours and tell you what we decided.
- **Legal basis:** legal obligation (handling notices of illegal content under the EU Digital Services Act) and legitimate interest in a safe service.
- **Kept:** the report is kept as a record of our moderation. Your email address is deleted 6 months after we decide the report.

### Moderation decisions

- **Data:** decisions to hide or remove your products or suspend your account, with the reasons, and anything you write to contest them.
- **Why:** to tell you why we restricted something, handle your contest and keep a record of our moderation.
- **Legal basis:** contract and legal obligation (statements of reasons under the EU Digital Services Act).
- **Kept:** while your account exists. If you delete your account, we keep the decision without your details or what you wrote, so our monthly moderation counts stay accurate.

### Ranking signals

- **Data:** a record that an account saved, claimed a perk on, clicked through to, gave feedback on or voted for a product, with the day it happened. Visits and clicks from people without an account are only counted as anonymous totals per product.
- **Why:** to rank products fairly and to detect manipulation. See [How your activity ranks products](#how-your-activity-ranks-products).
- **Legal basis:** legitimate interest in rankings that reflect what real people do and can't be gamed.
- **Kept:** the individual records are deleted after 30 days, or earlier if you delete your account. The daily totals per product contain no personal data and are kept.

### Makers

- **Data:** the domains you verify (with the verification token and method), the products you publish, the images you upload, the perks and codes you add, and a screenshot we take of your product's website. Uploaded images are stored with your account ID attached.
- **Why:** to verify that you own the product's website, publish your product page and deliver perks.
- **Legal basis:** contract.
- **Kept:** until you remove the product or domain, or delete your account. When you delete your account, your products and their images are deleted too. Image files can stay in storage after you remove a single product; email [PRIVACY_EMAIL] to have them deleted.

### Emails

- **Data:** your email address and the content of the message.
- **Why:** to confirm your email address and reset your password, and to send the [waitlist](#the-waitlist) emails.
- **Legal basis:** contract.
- **Kept:** we don't keep copies. Our email provider keeps delivery logs for a limited time.

### Server logs

- **Data:** for each request: time, address of the page, IP address, browser type and any error.
- **Why:** to fix errors and keep the service secure.
- **Legal basis:** legitimate interest.
- **Kept:** a few days, after which Cloudflare deletes them automatically.

## How your activity ranks products

Products are ranked by what people do with them, not by payment. When you save a product, claim a perk, click through to its website, send feedback or vote on its roadmap, that counts as one signal for the product.

- Signals count only from accounts with a confirmed email that are at least a few days old.
- Each person counts once per product and type of signal (clicks, feedback and votes at most once a day).
- A maker's activity on their own products never counts.
- Signals are added up per product and day. Rankings show only the result; nobody can see who contributed to them.

The full rules are on [How ranking works](/ranking). You can object to this use of your data (see [Your rights](#your-rights)).

## Who processes data for us

We use these providers to run the service. They process personal data only on our instructions.

- **Cloudflare, Inc.** (United States): hosting, database, file storage, image processing, bot checks (Turnstile), website screenshots and server logs.
- **Google LLC** (United States): sign-in with Google, only if you choose it. Profile pictures of people who signed in with Google are loaded from Google's servers, so Google receives the IP address of anyone viewing a page that shows one.
- **Resend** (United States): sending email, including the waitlist confirmation, invite and launch emails. Resend receives your email address and the content of each message.
- **Browser push services** (for example Google, Mozilla, Apple or Microsoft, depending on your browser): delivering push notifications, only if you turn them on. Message content is encrypted.

We don't sell personal data or share it for advertising. We may disclose data when the law requires it.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correct** data that is wrong or incomplete.
- **Delete** your data.
- **Take your data with you** (portability) in a machine-readable format.
- **Object** to processing based on legitimate interest, including the use of your activity for rankings.
- **Restrict** processing while a request is being handled.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, for example for push notifications, without affecting what was done before.
- **Complain** to a supervisory authority (see below).

**How to use them:**

- In [Settings](/settings) you can change your name, username, bio, the categories you follow, how often you get the digest, push notifications and your password.
- Under "Your data" in [Settings](/settings) you can download a copy of your data as a JSON file and delete your account. The download link works for 24 hours; the file is then deleted in the daily cleanup.
- For everything else, including an objection, email [PRIVACY_EMAIL] from the address on your account. We reply within one month and may ask you to confirm your identity.

**Complaints:** you can complain to the National Center for Personal Data Protection of the Republic of Moldova, or to the data protection authority of the EU country where you live, work or think your rights were breached. We'd appreciate the chance to fix the problem first, at [PRIVACY_EMAIL].
