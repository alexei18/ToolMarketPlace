## How ranking works

Products are ranked by what people do with them in a given period: saves, perk claims, clicks to the product's website, feedback and roadmap votes. Top this week looks at the last 7 days; Trending looks at the last 48 hours and favours what is picking up speed; New lists launches by date.

- Only confirmed accounts that are at least a few days old count, each person counts once per product, and makers' activity on their own products never counts.
- Our team picks a few Staff picks each week. They are always labelled and get a temporary boost in Top this week.
- Valid reports lower a product's position.
- **Nobody can pay for ranking.** No payment, partnership or relationship with us changes a product's position. If we ever show sponsored listings, they will be marked "Sponsored" and kept out of the rankings.

The full rules are on [How ranking works](/ranking).
