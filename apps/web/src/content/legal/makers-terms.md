These terms apply when you publish a product on Tryworthy. They add to the [Terms of Service](/terms) and the [Community Guidelines](/guidelines).

## Verifying your domain

Before you publish, you verify that you control the product's website, with a DNS record or a meta tag. You can only publish products hosted on a domain you have verified. If you lose control of the domain, remove the product or tell us at [CONTACT_EMAIL].

## Your product must be yours

By publishing, you confirm that:

- you made the product or are allowed to represent it;
- the listing is accurate: name, description, pricing, platforms, whether it includes AI features, and its stage;
- you have the rights to the text, images and logos you upload;
- the product and its website are legal where they are offered and don't contain malware.

## Responsibility for your product

You are responsible for your product, its website, its terms and how it handles its users' data. Tryworthy only lists it. If someone makes a claim against us because of your product or listing, you agree to help us deal with it and to cover the losses it causes us, as far as the law allows.

## Perks

Perks are your offer to Tryworthy members; we are not a party to them.

- Describe each perk clearly, with its conditions and expiry date.
- Honour every perk that is claimed while it is active, including codes you uploaded.
- Use codes that work and that you are allowed to give away. Don't charge for a perk that is described as free.
- If you can no longer honour a perk, turn it off in your dashboard.

Perks that aren't honoured can be removed, and repeated problems can lead to removal of the product.

## Ranking

Publishing is automatic; ranking decides only where a product appears. Your own activity on your products, and that of accounts that own the product's domain, never counts. To appear in Top this week, Trending and the digest, a product needs a working link, at least one image, a tagline, a full description and no open moderation case. Your dashboard shows what is missing. Nobody can pay for ranking. See [How ranking works](/ranking).

## Limits

To prevent spam, the number of products, uploads and changes per day is limited. The current limits are shown in your dashboard when you reach them. We can change them at any time.

## Reasons for removal

We can hide or remove a product, a perk or a maker account when:

- the product or website breaks the [Community Guidelines](/guidelines) or the law;
- you don't control the domain or don't have the right to list the product;
- the listing is misleading or the website no longer works;
- perks aren't honoured;
- you try to manipulate rankings, for example with fake accounts or paid signals;
- we receive a valid report of illegal content.

When we hide or remove a product or suspend your account, we tell you what we restricted, why, which rule applied and whether the decision was automated. You can contest it once within six months from "Decisions" in your dashboard, as described in the [Terms of Service](/terms#contesting-a-decision).

## Removing your product

You can remove your product from your dashboard at any time. Its page stops being public. Perks already claimed stay your responsibility until they expire.
