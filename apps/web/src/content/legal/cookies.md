ToolMarket uses only the cookies and browser storage needed for the site to work. We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, so we don't show a cookie banner. This follows Moldova's Law no. 284/2004 on electronic commerce and the EU ePrivacy rules, which allow strictly necessary cookies without consent.

## Cookies we set

All of them are strictly necessary. Visitors who don't sign in get no cookies from us.

### Session cookie

- **Name:** `better-auth.session_token` (`__Secure-better-auth.session_token` on HTTPS)
- **What it does:** keeps you signed in. Scripts on the page can't read it.
- **How long:** 30 days, renewed once a day while you use the site, or until you log out.

### Sign-in hint

- **Name:** `tm_auth`
- **What it does:** contains only "1". It tells pages that you are probably signed in, so they show the right menu without a flash. It holds no personal data.
- **How long:** 30 days, or until you log out.

### Google sign-in protection

- **Name:** `better-auth.state` (`__Secure-better-auth.state` on HTTPS)
- **What it does:** protects the sign-in with Google against forged requests. Set only while you sign in with Google.
- **How long:** 5 minutes.

## Other storage in your browser

### Saved products

- **Name:** `tm:saved-ids` (session storage)
- **What it does:** remembers which products you saved, so save buttons show the right state without asking the server on every page.
- **How long:** until you close the tab.

### Push notifications

- **Name:** the service worker `/sw.js` and the push subscription your browser creates for us
- **What it does:** shows the notifications you asked for. Both are set up only when you turn on push notifications; the service worker stores no data about you.
- **How long:** until you turn push off or clear the site's data.

### Nothing else

Apart from the items above, we don't use local storage, IndexedDB or any other browser storage. If you arrive from a link with campaign tags (`utm_source`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`), they stay in the page address and are saved on your account only if you sign up, as described in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy#where-you-came-from).

## Third-party services on our pages

- **Cloudflare Turnstile** runs a bot check from `challenges.cloudflare.com` when you sign up, claim a perk, report a product or start publishing one.
- **Google** shows its sign-in page when you choose "Continue with Google". Profile pictures of people who signed in with Google load from Google's servers, without telling Google which page you were on.
- **Cloudflare** hosts the site. If its bot protection checks your browser, it may set its own strictly necessary security cookies, `__cf_bm` (30 minutes) or `cf_clearance` (after a security check, usually 30 minutes).

We don't load analytics, advertising, fonts or scripts from any other service.

These services follow their own privacy policies. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy#who-processes-data-for-us) for what they receive.

## Managing cookies

You can delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. If you block or delete the cookies above, you will be signed out and won't be able to sign in until you allow them again.

If we ever add cookies that are not strictly necessary, we will update this page and ask for your consent first.
