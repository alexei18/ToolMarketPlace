## Copy deck (engleză)

Textele de mai jos sunt punctul de pornire pentru Claude Code; el le poate ajusta ca lungime, dar nu ca mesaj. Variantele A și B sunt pentru testare. Unde e cazul, textul de pre-lansare apare separat.

**Homepage — secțiunea „problema”**

- Titlu: *New launches every day. No way to tell which ones are good.*
- Text: *Launch boards reward whoever brings the most votes on day one. Social feeds reward whoever shouts loudest. The good stuff gets buried — and you find it months later, after everyone else.*

**Homepage — „cum funcționează” (3 pași)**

1. **Pick what you're into.** Productivity, design, health, dev — any of 20 categories.
2. **We rank every new launch.** By what real people save, try and comment on. Makers can't pay to move up.
3. **You get there first.** A weekly top in your categories, early access to betas, and perks made for you.

**Homepage — bloc pentru creatori**

- Titlu: *Make content about tools before they trend.*
- Text: *Trending shows what's picking up speed this week. Pre-launch betas let you try things nobody has covered yet.*
- CTA: *See what's trending*

**Homepage — bloc pentru makeri**

- După lansare: *Built something? Get it in front of people who actually try new apps.* · CTA: *List your product — free*
- Pre-lansare: *Launching something? Join as a founding maker — your product goes into the launch-day digest.* · CTA: *Submit your product*

**Pagina „/makers”** (adăugat 2026-10-29, aprobat la pasul 7; H1 și CTA sunt cele din blocul pentru makeri, „How ranking works” e textul paginii `/ranking`)

- De ce aici: *Why list here* · *People come here to find new apps before everyone else. List yours and it reaches them in the categories they follow — in their feed and in their weekly top.* · *It's free: verify your domain, paste your link, and you're live.*
- Perks: *Perks* · *Offer an extended trial, a discount or a code that people can only claim here. Every claim brings you a signed-up user, and claims count toward your ranking.*
- Testare înainte de lansare: *Pre-launch testing* · *Not launched yet? List your product as Pre-launch and find your first testers. They can send feedback straight to you, in public or in private, and vote on your roadmap.*

- Tab-uri: *Top this week* · *Trending* · *New*
- Explicație lângă tab-uri: *Ranked by saves, perk claims and feedback from verified accounts. Makers can't pay to move up.* · link: *How ranking works*
- Badge-uri: *Staff pick* · *AI-powered* · *Just launched* · *Founding maker* · *Pre-launch*
- Filtre: *AI-powered* · *Free / Freemium / Paid* · *Pre-launch / Launched* · *Has a perk* · *Web / iOS / Android / Extension / Desktop*

**Onboarding**

- Titlu: *What are you into?*
- Subtitlu: *Pick at least 3. We'll rank what's new in each.*
- Pas digest: *How often do you want your top picks?* · Daily · Weekly (recommended) · Only in the app (= fără digest; decis la pasul 7, D11)
- Final (după lansare): *Done. Here's what's worth trying this week.*
- Final (pre-lansare): *You're in. On launch day you'll get this week's top picks in your categories.*

**Pagina de categorie (șablon)**

- H1: *New \[Category\] apps worth trying — this week*
- Intro: *Every new launch in \[category\], including AI-powered tools, ranked by real signals. Updated daily.*
- Banner pentru vizitatori (după limită): *You've seen this week's top \[category\] picks. Create a free account to see every launch and get the weekly top in your notifications.*

**Pagina produsului — microcopy**

- Badge perk: *Only on Tryworthy*
- Buton perk pentru vizitator: *Sign in to claim this perk*
- Badge beta: *Pre-launch — try it before everyone else*
- Feedback: *Tell the maker what would make this a must-have.*

**Pagina „How ranking works” (`/ranking`)**

*Top this week ranks new launches by what people did with them in the last 7 days: saves, perk claims, visits to the product and feedback. Trending looks at the last 48 hours and favors what is picking up speed. Only verified accounts older than a few days count, each person counts once per product, and makers' own activity doesn't count. Our team adds a few Staff picks each week, always labeled. Nobody can pay to rank higher; if we ever show sponsored listings, they will be marked "Sponsored" and kept out of the ranking.*

**Notificări digest**

- Titlu: *Top 5 new in \[Category\] this week*
- Când nu e nimic nou: nu se trimite nimic.
- Ziua lansării (o singură dată): *We're live. Here's what's worth trying in your categories.*

**Pagina „About” (paragraful de bază)**

*Tryworthy exists because the best new apps get buried. Every day people ship apps — some built in a weekend, some with AI at their core — and most never reach the people who'd love them. We rank every new launch by what real people save, try and talk about, so you find the good ones first, and makers get seen for what they build, not for how loud they launch.*
